Why LicenseDesktop

A complete licensing toolkit that stays under your control — keys are signed on your machine, not in the cloud.

Offline

100% Offline Signing

Never leak your master keys to a third-party server. Activation codes are signed locally on your machine — your keys stay yours.

Versions

Tiered Editions

Manage Basic, Professional and Enterprise tiers with per-feature gating. Upgrade users without touching your code.

Lifecycle

Complete License Lifecycle

Issue, verify, extend, re-key and revoke licenses in a few clicks. Trials and expiry downgrade are built in.

Purchase

Online purchase

Buy a license right inside the app — Alipay (CNY) or overseas credit card (USD). Clear pricing, instant activation.

Updates

Auto updates

Stay current without lifting a finger. New versions are delivered automatically, and you can always download updates manually if needed.

Backups

Automatic backups

Sleep easy. Automatic local backups, optional encrypted off-site copies, and restore from the cloud — your data is never locked in.
