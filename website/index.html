Why LicenseDesktop

A complete licensing toolkit that stays under your control — keys are signed on your machine, not in the cloud.

Offline

Offline signing

Activation codes are RSA/JWT-signed locally on your machine. No key escrow, no cloud dependency.

Versions

Edition-based licensing

Basic, Professional and Enterprise editions with per-feature gating — control what each tier unlocks.

Lifecycle

Full lifecycle

Issue, verify, extend, re-key and revoke licenses. Trial support and expiry downgrade built in.

Central + Publisher

Two roles, one toolkit

A Central edition for your own operations and a Publisher edition you distribute — cleanly separated.

Updates

Auto updates

Version-gated automatic updates via CDN, with manual fallback and maintenance-window control.

Backups

Automatic backups

Scheduled, rolling backups with optional encrypted off-site copies. Your data is never locked in.
